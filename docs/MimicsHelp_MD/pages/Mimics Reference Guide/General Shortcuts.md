### General Shortcuts

F1 |  Open the Online Help  
---|---  
CTRL + Z |  Undo last action  
CTRL + Y |  Redo last action  
CTRL + F |  Enable/Disable the Navigation Toolbar  
CTRL + I |  Makes all mask invisible  
CTRL + SHIFT + M |  Enable/Disable the Movie export  
CTRL + SHIFT + Printscreen |  Copies a screenshot of the active view to the clipboard. From there it can easily be pasted into another application.  
CTRL + SHIFT + P |  Takes a screenshot of the active view and saves it as Jpeg in the project folder.  
Alt + F4 |  Exit Mimics  
SPACE |  If you hover with the mouse over a view and press the spacebar; that view is put to full screen. To unzoom to full screen; press the spacebar
