### Distance Over the Surface  
  
This tool is designed to measure the shortest distance over the surface of a 3D model. To use this tool select the icon ![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/020000F2_44x37.jpg) from the Measure toolbar or go to Measure > Distance Over the Surface in the menu.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Measure_SurfaceDistance_new.png)

The tool will change into a pencil. Indicate several points that indicate the path you want the measurement to follow. The measurement is expressed in mm and shown on the screen. Fine adjustments can be made to the measurement by dragging one of the points to a new location. 

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/distance%20on%20surface1_331x183.png) |  ![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/distance%20on%20surface2_298x185.png)  
---|---  
  
To show its context menu, right-click on one of the end points.
