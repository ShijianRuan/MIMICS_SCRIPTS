### Cavity Fill

Fills internal gaps of the selected mask and places this in a new mask.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/CavityFill.png)

Source and target masks are set here. The multiple layer checkbox indicates if you will fill in 3D or just in 2D.

Select the cavity to fill by a click of the left mouse button (bucket shaped).

Please be aware that if the cavity you are trying to fill is not covered by the mask on all sides, the mask will spill to the surrounding area. Therefore, it is best to duplicate your mask before running this operation so you have a copy. You can also edit the mask using the mask editing tools to obtain a closed mask.
