#### Place Distractor

To place a distractor, select the corresponding option from **Simulate > Distraction** in the menu.

You can place a distractor on an existing object or on a part of an object. For this, you first need to cut and split.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/02000258_276x270.jpg)

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/02000259_276x262.jpg)

By selecting 'Place Distractor' from the Simulate menu the following window will appear:

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/0300025A_809x110.png)

Functions on Objects to place the distractor on

Visible |  Lists if the object is visible or not by means of glasses. Click on the glasses to change the visibility of the object.  
---|---  
Contour visible |  Lists if the contour of the object is visible or not by means of glasses. Click on the glasses to change the visibility of the contour of the object.  
Show selected only |  Makes the selected objects visible and the unselected objects invisible.  
  
Select on which part you want to place your distractor and click Next. A distractor library will appear in which you can chose which distractor you want to place.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/0300025B_709x396.png)

Locate the distractor with two mouse clicks in the 3D view. The first click determines the point of the distractor on the fixed part of the bone, the second mouse click determines the point of the distractor on the moveable part of the bone.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/0200025C_325x264.jpg)

##### Adjust the Distractor Position

The direction of the distraction vector can be adjusted with the following window. The distractor can also be aligned to the sagittal or axial slices or to the Frankfurt plane.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/0300025D_1007x141.png)

You can also change the vector of the distractor by left-clicking on the red arrow originated from the distractor and dragging the arrow.
