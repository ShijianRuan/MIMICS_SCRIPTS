#### Quality  
  
When you calculate a Part you can choose the quality. Take low or medium quality if you want a short calculation time, but the dimensions of the Part will not be accurate because of the matrix reduction that is applied on the images! You will get the most accurate result when you select the optimal quality setting. The high quality setting will still do a matrix reduction in the XY plane but can give in some situations a smoother and better looking Part. Only when you select custom quality you can choose the Part calculation parameters yourself by selecting the Custom radio button.

The recommended quality (according to your computer) will be highlighted with a *.
