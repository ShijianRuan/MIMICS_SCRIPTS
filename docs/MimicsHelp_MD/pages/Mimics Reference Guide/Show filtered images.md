### Show filtered images

By selecting or unselecting Show filtered images, you can switch between the view before and after applying the filters.

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/03000105_224x199.png) |  ![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/03000106_224x199.png)  
---|---  
Show filtered images off |  Show filtered images on  
  
Note: If you reselect Show filtered images, the filtering operations will be reapplied to the image set. This operation may take a few minutes.
