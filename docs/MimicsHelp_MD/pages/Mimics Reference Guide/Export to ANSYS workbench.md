#### Export to ANSYS workbench

When you obtain your volume mesh go to the Export menu and choose ANSYS. This will open following interface:

![](Mimics Reference Guide/../Resources/Images/Mimics%2015.0%20Reference%20Guide_track_v2_no_numbers/03000214_474x429.png)

Select your volume mesh to be exported, set your Output Format as ANSYS Preprocessor files, click Add and click on the OK button.
